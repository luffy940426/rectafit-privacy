Data requests

Effective: October 16, 2026

Request deletion or access

Email trandacthe94@gmail.com with the subject "RectaFit privacy request". Include Android or iOS, approximate dates, and what you want deleted or accessed. Do not send passwords, financial information, or private files.

What can be removed

Local game data: clear RectaFit app storage on your device. This removes progress, your unfinished board, settings, and tutorial status. Operating-system backups are controlled by your platform settings.

Optional reports: turn off Settings > Share usage & crash reports to stop future reporting. For previously uploaded data, email us. We use available Firebase/Analytics deletion tools when we can identify the relevant record. The game has no account, so an email address alone may not identify an installation. We will explain any additional information needed and any limits.

Retention and limits

We may need limited details to verify your request. Aggregated statistics may not identify an individual. Provider backup deletion schedules and legal retention obligations may apply. Turning off sharing does not automatically delete reports already uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.
