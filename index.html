Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how RectaFit: Block Puzzle (RectaFit), published by RectaFit developer (GitHub: luffy940426), handles information when you play the game or contact us.

Data collection

RectaFit works offline and does not require an account. Optional usage analytics and crash reporting are off by default. You can enable or disable them using Settings > Share usage & crash reports. You can play the complete game without enabling this setting. The current version has no advertising, purchases, or cloud saves.

Data stored on your device

Progress, your unfinished board, settings, and tutorial status are stored on your device. Any existing daily-challenge preferences are also stored locally. These records are not uploaded as a saved game by RectaFit. Device or operating-system backup and restore features may handle app data under their own settings.

Optional usage and crash reports

When sharing is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay and app interaction events: level identifiers and numbers, campaign, starts, resumes, completions, exits, resets, replay and Perfect status, moves, hints, and play time. The SDK also processes app-instance identifiers, sessions, screen views, app and device information, and approximate location inferred from network information. We use this to understand difficulty and improve the game.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and handled-error reports, stack traces, exception messages, app state, app version, device and operating-system details, installation identifiers, and session diagnostics. Gameplay events may appear as breadcrumbs in crash reports. We use these reports to find and fix faults. We do not attach your name, email address, or an account ID to gameplay or crash reports. These identifiers are pseudonymous; they should not be treated as fully anonymous.

Permissions and advertising

RectaFit does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or GPS location. Approximate location in analytics does not require GPS permission. Advertising ID collection and advertising consent are disabled in the app. We do not sell personal information or use gameplay data for targeted advertising. Sound and vibration run on your device.

Service providers, security, and international processing

Optional reports are processed by Google through Firebase and Google Analytics. They are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Google and its service providers may process data outside your country. Access to our reporting tools is restricted to people maintaining the game. No system is completely secure. Google Play and the App Store handle their own store activity under their policies.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How long information is kept

Local game data remains until you clear it, remove the app, or it is replaced through normal game use, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Analytics user-level and event-level retention follows the property setting: standard Google Analytics properties offer 2 or 14 months, and new activity may renew the retention period for an identifier. Aggregated reports can remain longer. Contact us for the current property setting. Firebase retains Crashlytics reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.

Support correspondence is kept while handling your request and only as long afterwards as needed for follow-up, security, or legal obligations. We do not promise that switching off sharing immediately erases information already received by a provider.

Your choices and deletion requests

Turn off Settings > Share usage & crash reports to stop future optional reporting. The app requests deletion of unsent Crashlytics reports and resets local Analytics data; this does not erase reports already uploaded. Clear app storage to remove local progress and preferences.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold, email trandacthe94@gmail.com with the subject "RectaFit privacy request". Tell us the platform, approximate dates, and what you want removed. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private files. We may ask for limited additional information to locate the relevant record and verify the request. Because the game has no account, we cannot always connect an email address to an installation or identify an individual in aggregate reports. We will explain any limits and use available provider deletion tools where the record can be identified. Legal retention and provider backup-deletion schedules may apply.

You may also have the right to withdraw consent or complain to your local data protection authority, depending on where you live.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and the details you choose to include. We use them to answer your question or handle a privacy request. This correspondence is separate from in-app analytics. Please avoid publishing personal information in public GitHub issues.

Children

RectaFit is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact trandacthe94@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Policy changes

We will update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes requiring new consent will be handled before the affected optional collection begins.
